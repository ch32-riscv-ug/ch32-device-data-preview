# Chapter 5 System Timer (SysTick)

QingKe V4 series microprocessor is designed with a 64-bit plus counter (SysTick) inside, and its clock  
source can be the system clock or 8 divisions of the system clock. It can provide time base for real time  
operating system, provide timing, measure time, etc. The timer involves 6 registers and maps to the  
peripheral address space for controlling the SysTick, as shown in Table 5-1 below.
