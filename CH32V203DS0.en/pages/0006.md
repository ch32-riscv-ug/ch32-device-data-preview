# Chapter 2 Specification

CH32V203 series are 32-bit RISC core MCUs based on the RISC-V instruction set architecture (ISA), with  
144MHz operating frequency, and built-in high-speed memory. It has multiple buses working synchronously,  
and provides a wealth of peripheral functions and enhanced I/O ports. This series of products has built-in 2  
12-bit ADC modules, multiple timers, multi-channel capacitance touch key detection (TKey) and other  
functions. It also contains standard and dedicated communication interfaces: I²C, SPI, USART, CAN  
controller, USB2.0 full-speed host/device controller, USB2.0 full-speed device controller, etc.  
The rated working voltage of the product is 3.3V. It supports several power-saving operating modes to meet  
the product&#x27;s low-power application requirements. Various models in the series are different in terms of  
resource allocation, number of peripherals, peripheral functions, etc., and can be selected as needed.
