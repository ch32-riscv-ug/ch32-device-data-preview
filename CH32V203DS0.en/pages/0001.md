# CH32V203 Datasheet

V3.0  

# Overview

CH32V series are industrial-grade general-purpose microcontrollers designed based on QingKe 32-bit RISC-V.  
The entire series of products incorporates designs such as hardware stack area and fast interrupt entry, which  
improves interrupt response speed compared to traditional MCUs. CH32V203 is an industrial-grade enhanced  
low-power general-purpose microcontroller designed based on the 32-bit RISC-V core. It is equipped with the  
QingKe V4B core. The work and sleep power consumption per MHz has dropped significantly year-on-year. It  
supports 144MHz main frequency zero-wait high-performance operation, and the running performance/power  
consumption ratio far exceeds traditional MCUs. CH32V203 series integrated dual USB interface, support USB  
Host and USB Device function, with CAN interface (2.0B active), dual OPA, 4 groups of USART, dual I2C,  
12-bit ADC, 10-channel Touchkey and other rich peripheral resources.
