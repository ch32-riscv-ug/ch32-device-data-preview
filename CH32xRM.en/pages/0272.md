<em>USBD_ADDRx_TX is mapped to USBD_ADDRx_RX_0</em>  
<em>USBD_ADDRx_RX is mapped to USBD_ADDRx_RX_1</em>  
<em>USBD_COUNTx_TX is mapped to USB_COUNTx_RX_0</em>  
<em>USBD_COUNTx_RX is mapped to USB_COUNTx_RX_1</em>  
The higher 6 bits of the USBD_COUNTx_RX register define the size of the receiving packet buffer so that the  
USBD module can detect the overflow boundary of the buffer. The size of the buffer can be expressed  
according to the parameter maxPacketSize in the endpoint descriptor in the device enumeration process.
