### 11.2.3 DMA Request Mapping

The DMA controller provides 7 channels, and each channel corresponds to multiple peripheral requests. By  
setting the corresponding DMA control bit in the corresponding peripheral register, the DMA function of each  
peripheral can be switched on or off independently.
