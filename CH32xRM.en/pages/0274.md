<table><caption>Table 21-2 USB system control combination</caption><tr><th>RB_UC_HOST_MODE</th><th>MASK_UC_SYS_CTRL</th><th>USB system control description</th></tr><tr><td>0</td><td>00</td><td style="text-align:left">Disable the USB device function and disable the pull-up resistor.</td></tr><tr><td>0</td><td>01</td><td style="text-align:left">Enable the USB device function and disable the internal pull-up resistor.
