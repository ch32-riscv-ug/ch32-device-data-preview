### 10.2.11 Alternate Function Remapping GPIO Settings


#### 10.2.11.1 OSC_IN/OSC_OUT as GPIO port PD0/PD1

OSC_IN/OSC_OUT can be used as GPIO PD0/PD1, which is realized by setting Remap Register 1  
(AFIO_PCFR).
