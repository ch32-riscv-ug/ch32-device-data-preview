# Chapter 16 Digital-to-analog Converter (DAC)

<strong><em>This chapter applies to the whole family of CH32F103.</em></strong>  
Digital-to-analog converter (DAC), including a 12-bit digital input converter to convert 2-channel analog  
voltage outputs. Built-in triangle wave and noise waveform generator, supporting a variety of event trigger  
conversion, DMA function, etc.  

## 16.1 Main Features

● 1 DAC converter corresponds to 2 channels of monotonic output  
● Triangular-wave, noise-wave generation  
● Left or right data alignment in 12-bit mode  
● DMA capability  
● Multiple trigger events
