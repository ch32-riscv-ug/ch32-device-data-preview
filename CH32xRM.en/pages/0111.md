### 12.2.2 ADC Configuration

<strong>1) Power-on</strong>  
The ADON bit in the ADC_CTLR2 register is 1, indicating that the ADC module is powered on. When the ADC  
module enters the power-on status (ADON=1) from the power-down mode (ADON=0), it needs to delay a  
period of time t as the module stabilization time. Afterwards, write the ADON bit as 1 again, to serve as the  
STAB
