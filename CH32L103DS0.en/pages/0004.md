# Chapter 1 Specification Information


## 1.1 System Structure

The microcontroller is designed on the basis of the RISC-V instruction set, and its architecture integrates QingKe  
microprocessor core, arbitration unit, DMA module, SRAM storage and other components through multiple bus  
groups to achieve interaction. A general-purpose DMA controller is integrated to reduce the CPU load and  
improve access efficiency, and a multi-level clock management mechanism is applied to reduce the power  
consumption of peripherals. The following diagram shows the overall internal architecture of the series chip.
