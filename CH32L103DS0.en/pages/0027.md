<em>For chips where the BOOT0 pin is brought out but the BOOT1/PB2 pin is not, the internal BOOT1/PB2 pin is</em>  
<em>internally shorted to GND. In low-power conditions, it is recommended to configure PB2 as a pull-down input to</em>  
<em>prevent additional current flow.</em>  
<em>Note 6: For CH32L103K8U chips, BOOT0 and PB9 pins are short sealed inside the chip, and PB9 pins no longer</em>  
<em>support voltage 5V; for CH32L103F8P6 chips, BOOT0 and PB8 pins are short sealed inside the chip, and PB8</em>  
<em>pins no longer support voltage 5V. It is recommended to connect an external 4.7K pull-down resistor to ensure</em>  
<em>that the BOOT0 is low during power-up in order to enter the program flash memory bootstrap mode. After normal</em>  
<em>operation, PB9 and PB8 pins can be used for output as needed.</em>  
<em>Note 7: For the CH32L103G8R6 chip, the PA14 and PB5 pins are short sealed inside the chip, so it is forbidden</em>  
<em>to configure both IO as the output function; when the PB1 and PB10 pins are short sealed inside the chip, the</em>  
<em>PB10 pin no longer supports voltage tolerant 5V, and it is forbidden to configure both IO as the output function.</em>  
<em>The PA12 and PA13 pins are short-connected and sealed inside the chip, so it is forbidden to configure both IO as</em>  
<em>the output function.</em>  
<em>Note 8: For the CH32L103F8U6 chip, the PB1 and PB10 pins are shorted and sealed inside the chip, so the PB10</em>  
<em>pin no longer supports voltage 5V, and both IO are prohibited from being configured as the output function; the</em>  
<em>PB6 and PB13 pins are shorted and sealed inside the chip, and both IO are prohibited from being configured as</em>  
<em>the output function; PA12 and PA14 pins are shorted inside the chip, and both IO are prohibited from being</em>  
<em>configured as the output function. The PA11 and PA13 pins are short-connected and sealed inside the chip, so it is</em>  
<em>forbidden to configure both IO as the output function, while the PA10 and PB7 pins are short-connected and</em>  
<em>sealed inside the chip, so it is forbidden to configure both IO as the output function.</em>  
<em>Note 9: For the CH32L103F8P6 chip, PA13 and PB6 pins are short-connected and sealed inside the chip, and</em>  
<em>both IO are prohibited from being configured as output functions; PA14 and PB7 pins are short-connected and</em>  
<em>sealed inside the chip, and both IO are prohibited from being configured as output functions.</em>  
<em>Note 10: The underlined value of the remapping function indicates the configuration value of the corresponding</em>  
<em>bit in the AFIO register. For example, CAN1_RX_2 indicates that the corresponding bit configuration of the AFIO</em>  
<em>register is 10b.</em>
