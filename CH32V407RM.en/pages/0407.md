### 24.2.3 Clock

The clock pin of SDIO is SDIO_CK, and its output clock is obtained by dividing the frequency of HCLK, and  
the frequency dividing coefficient can be configured as any integer value between 2 and 261. SDIO devices  
generally only support a single bus mode with a clock of up to 400kHz during initialization. After initialization,  
the host generally initiates a switch to a low voltage operation, and at the same time increases the clock to the  
maximum clock that both the microcontroller and the SDIO device can accept. Different versions and speed  
grades of SD cards support different clock speeds and switching processes, and users need to understand for  
themselves.
