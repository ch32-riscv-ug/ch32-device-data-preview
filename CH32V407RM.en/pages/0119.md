<em>Note: The LCD-TFT controller pin must be configured through the user program. Unused pins can be used for</em>  
<em>other functions.</em>  
<em>For LTDC outputs up to 24 bits (RGB888), when outputting RGB565 or RGB666 to 16-bit or 18-bit displays</em>  
<em>using a pixel depth lower than 8bpp, the RGB display data lines must be connected to the MSB of the LCD-</em>  
<em>TFT controller&#x27;s RGB data lines. For example, when the LCD-TFT controller is connected to an RGB565 16-</em>  
<em>bit display, the LCD display&#x27;s R[4:0], G[4:0], and B[4:0] data line pins must connect to the LCD-TFT</em>  
<em>controller&#x27;s LCD_R[7:3], LCD_G[7:3], and LCD_B[7:3] pins.</em>
