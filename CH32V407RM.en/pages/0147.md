### 11.2.3 DMA Request Mapping

The DMA controller provides 24 channels, of which DMA1 contains 13 channels and DMA2 contains 11  
channels. Each channel corresponds to multiple peripheral requests. By setting the corresponding DMA control  
bit in the corresponding peripheral register, the DMA function of each peripheral can be independently turned  
on or off. The specific correspondence is as follows.
