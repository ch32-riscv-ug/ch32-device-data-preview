<strong>2) Sample clock</strong>  
The register operation of the module is based on the PCLK2 (PB2 bus) clock. The clock reference ADCCLK  
of the conversion unit is synchronized with the PCLK2. The frequency division is configured by the  
ADCPRE[1:0] domain of the RCC_CFGR0 register, and the maximum cannot exceed 30MHz.  
<strong>3) Channel configuration</strong>  
The ADC module provides 18 channel sampling sources, including 16 external channels and 2 internal  
channels. They can be configured into 2 translation groups: rule groups and injection groups. To realize the  
group conversion consisting of a series of transformations in any order on any number of channels.  
Conversion group:  
● Rule group: consists of up to 16 transformations. Regular channels and their conversion order are set in  
the ADCx_RSQRx register. The total number of translations in the rule group should be written to L[3:0]  
in the ADCx_RSQR1 register.  
● Injection group: composed of up to 4 transformations. The injection channels and their conversion order  
are set in the ADCx_ISQR register. The total number of translations in the injection group should be  
written to JL[1:0] in the ADCx_ISQR register.  
<em>Note: If the ADCx_RSQRx or ADCx_ISQR register is changed during the conversion, the current conversion</em>  
<em>is terminated and a new startup signal will be sent to ADC to convert the newly selected group.</em>  
2 internal channels:  
● Temperature sensor: Connect the ADC_IN16 channel to measure the internal temperature(TA) of the chip.  
● VREFINT internal reference voltage: Connect the ADC_IN17 channel.  
<strong>4) Calibration</strong>  
Initialize the calibration register by writing the RSTCAL bit of the ADCx_CTLR2 register to 1, and wait for  
the RSTCAL hardware to clear to 0 to indicate that the initialization is complete. Set the CAL bit to start the  
calibration function. Once the calibration is completed, the hardware will automatically clear the CAL bit.  
Perform offset calibration. After that, the normal conversion function can be started, and the offset value is  
added after each ADC conversion. It is recommended to perform an ADC calibration when the ADC module  
is powered on.  
<em>Note: Before starting the calibration, you must ensure that the ADC module is powered on (ADON=1) for at</em>  
<em>least 2 ADC clock cycles.</em>  
<strong>5) Programmable sample time</strong>  
ADC uses several ADCCLK cycles to sample the input voltage, and the number of sampling cycles of the  
channels can be changed by the SMPx[2:0] bit in the ADCx_SAMPTR1 and ADCx_SAMPTR2 registers.  
Each channel can be sampled at different times.  
The total conversion time is calculated as follows:  
TCONV= Sampling time + 12.5TADCCLK  
The sampling time can be determined based on SMPx[2:0].
