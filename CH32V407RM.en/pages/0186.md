# Chapter 13 Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC)

Digital/analog conversion module (DAC), including two 12-bit voltage output digital/analog converters  
(DAC), converts 2 digital signals into 2 analog voltage signals and outputs them, supports independent or  
synchronous conversion of dual DAC channels, supports 12-bit data left or right alignment, supports 12-bit or  
8-bit data, and supports external event-triggered conversion. Triangular wave and noise generation can be  
achieved. Support DMA function.  

## 13.1 Main Features

● 2 DAC converters, and each has single output channel  
● Triangular-wave generator and noise-wave generator  
● Configurable 8-bit or 12-bit output  
● Left or right data alignment in 12-bit mode  
● Dual DAC channel for independent or simultaneous conversions  
● DMA capability  
● Multiple trigger events
