from the channel x pin, it can be selected as TIx (the source of TI1 may be more than CH1. See the timer  
structure block diagram 14-1). Take TI1 as example, TI1 passes through the filter (ICF[3:0]) to generate TI1F,  
and then is divided into TI1F_Rising and TI1F_Falling after passing through the edge detector. These 2 signals
