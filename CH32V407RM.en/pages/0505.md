For example, the R32_LTDC_BPCR register is configured as 0x000E0005 (AHBP[11:0] is 0xE), and the  
R32_LTDC_AWCR register is configured as 0x028E01E5 (AAW[11:0] is 0x28E). To configure the horizontal  
position of a 630x460 window (with a horizontal starting offset of 5 pixels within the valid data area).  
1) First pixel of the layer window: WHSTPOS[11:0] should be programmed to 0x14 (0xE + 1 + 0x5);  
2) Last pixel of the layer window: WHSPPOS[11:0] should be programmed to 0x28A.
