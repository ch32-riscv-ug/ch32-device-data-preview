### 15.2.1 Overview

As shown in Figure 15-1, the structure of the general-purpose timer can be roughly divided into three parts,  
namely the input clock part, the core counter part and the compare capture channel part.h  
The clock for the general-purpose timer can come from the PB1 bus clock (CK_INT), from the external clock  
input pin (TIMx_ETR), from other timers with clock output (ITRx), and from the input of the compare capture  
channel (TIMx_CHx). These input clock signals become CK_PSC clocks after various set filtering and  
dividing operations, etc., and are output to the core counter section. In addition, these complex clock sources  
can also be output as TRGO to other peripherals such as timers, ADCs.  
The core of the general-purpose timer is a 16-bit counter (CNT). After CK_PSC is divided by the prescaler  
(PSC), it becomes CK_CNT and is finally lost to CNT. CNT supports up counting mode, down counting mode
