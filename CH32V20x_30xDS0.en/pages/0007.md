<em>Note: 1. The products with 256K FLASH+64K SRAM support user select word to be configured as one of</em>  
<em>several combinations of (192K FLASH+128K SRAM), (224K FLASH+96K SRAM), (256K FLASH+64K</em>  
<em>SRAM), (288K FLASH+32K SRAM). On this basis, the 256K FLASH+64K SRAM product with the sixth</em>  
<em>inverted lot number not equal to 0 has also added a configuration combination: (128K FLASH+192K SRAM).</em>  
<em>FLASH flash represents the zero-waiting running area R0WAIT, and the product of 256K FLASH+64K SRAM</em>  
<em>supports the non-zero waiting area of (480K- R0WAIT) bytes.</em>  
<em>2. The functions related to pin signals such as PWM and capture in the timer need to be combined with</em>  
<em>the pins packaged in the actual chip, and some packaged chips cannot be used without being led out.</em>  
<em>3. The CH32V303RCT7 chip supports an operating temperature range: -40°C~105°C.</em>
