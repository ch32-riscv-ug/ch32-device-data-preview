### 2.5.14 Digital-to-analog Converter (DAC)

The product has 2 built-in 12-bit voltage output digital / analog converters (DAC), which converts 2 digital  
signals into 2 analog voltage signals and outputs. It supports dual DAC channels independent or synchronous  
conversion, and supports external event trigger conversion. The trigger source includes the internal signal of  
the on-chip timer and the external pin (EXTI line 9). Triangular wave and noise generation can be realized.  
Support the use of DMA operations.  

### 2.5.15 Timer and Watchdog

The timers in the system include advanced timers, general timers, basic timers, watchdog timers, and system  
time base timers. The number of timers included in different products in the series is different, please refer to  
Table 2-2 for details.
