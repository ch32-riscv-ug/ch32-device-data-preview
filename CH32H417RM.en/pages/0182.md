### 10.2.3 DMA Request Multiplexer

The DMAMUX multiplexer reconfigures (routes) DMA request lines between the chip&#x27;s peripherals and the DMA  
controller. This routing capability is achieved through a programmable multi-channel DMA request line  
multiplexer. Each channel can either select a DMA request line without restriction or synchronize its selection  
with events from its DMAMUX synchronous input.  
The DMA controller provides 16 channels, where DMAMUX channels 1 through 8 are connected to DMA1  
channels 1 through 8, and DMAMUX channels 9 through 16 are connected to DMA2 channels 1 through 8.
