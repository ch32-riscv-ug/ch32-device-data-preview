### 23.4.7 Transmit CRC Register (SPIx_TCRCR) (x=1/2/3/4)

Offset address: 0x18

Stores the calculated result of the CRC check of the bytes that have been sent.<br>Setting CRCEN will reset this register. The calculation method uses the polynomial used by CRCPOLY.
