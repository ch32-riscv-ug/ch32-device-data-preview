### 32.3.4 Data Transfer

Data transfer is carried out on the data line SDIO_D, which has 3 widths of 1/4/8 bits. During data transfer, the  
start bit of a clock is generally at the front, the high bit of each byte is in the front, and the low bit is in the back.  
For the block transfer mode only supported by the SD card, the data transfer of each block is followed by a CRC.  
Enable the RANDOM_LEN_EN bit, configure the byte length indicated by DBLOCKSIZE2, configure the total  
length of the transferred data indicated by R32_SDIO_DLEN, enable the DTEN bit, and fill in the data to the  
R32_SDIO_FIFO, then you can complete the transfer of the block of any byte length with CRC checksum.  
The MMC card also supports the data streaming transfer mode, at this time no CRC is attached. the following  
figure shows the format of the data transfer.
