# Chapter 47 Universal High-speed Interface (UHSIF)

<strong><em>The module description in this chapter only applies to CH32H417 microcontroller products.</em></strong>  
The chip incorporates a universal high-speed interface (UHSIF) capable of operating at a maximum transmission  
clock frequency of 125MHz. It supports 8-bit, 16-bit, or 32-bit data widths, with a theoretical maximum data  
transfer rate of 500Mbytes/s.  
For specific applications, please refer to and utilize the provided subroutine library.
