<em>Note: The LSEON, LSEBYP, CCO, ASOE, ASOS, RTCSEL, RTCEN and RTCCAL bits within the Backup Domain</em>  
<em>Control Register (RCC_BDCTLR) reside in the backup domain. Consequently, these bits remain in write-protected</em>  
<em>state after reset and can only be modified after setting the DBP bit to 1 in the Power Control Register (PWR_CR).</em>  
<em>These bits can be cleared by a backup domain reset. Neither internal nor external resets affect these bits.</em>
