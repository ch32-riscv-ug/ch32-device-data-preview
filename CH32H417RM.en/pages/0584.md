#### 27.2.6.10 U3 Wake-up LFPS Duration Configuration Register (R8_LINK_U3_WKUP_FILTER)

Offset address: 0x30

#### 27.2.6.11 U3 Wake-up LFPS Duration Configuration Register (R16_LINK_ISO_DLY)

<!-- p584-table-003 (table-27-18@1) -->
