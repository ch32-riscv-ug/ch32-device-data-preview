● Pulse output  
Configure the ASOE bit in the RCC_BDCTLR register to turn on the RTC pulse output, set the ASOS bit, and  
select whether the second pulse output or the alarm pulse output.  
● RTC Calibration  
When the CCO bit of the RCC_BDCTLR register is configured, the internal RTC clock is divided by 64 and  
output to the PC13 pin. Through actual testing, software adjusts the clock to calibrate the RTC in conjunction with  
modifying the RTCCAL[6:0] bits.  

### 3.3.8 BKP Interface Reset

The BKP region can be powered independently by VBAT during a VDD33 main power supply failure. During  
application code-controlled reset of the BKP region registers, the ASOS and ASOE bits are reset under the  
BDRST bit of the software-configured RCC_BDCTLR register and are unaffected by the BKPRST bit of the RCC  
peripheral interface control.
