<strong>DFSDM Clock</strong>  
The internal DFSDM clock fDFSDMCLK drives channel transceivers, digital processing modules (including digital  
filters and integrators), and subsequent auxiliary modules (such as analog watchdogs, short-circuit detectors,  
extreme-value detectors, and control modules). This clock is generated by the RCC module, sourced from either  
the peripheral clock HCLK or the audio clock. The DFSDM clock automatically stops in stop mode (when all  
DFSDM_FLTx, x=0/1, and DFEN=0).  
The DFSDM serial channel transceiver can accept an external serial clock to sample external serial data streams.  
If standard SPI encoding is used, the internal DFSDM clock must be at least four times faster than the external  
serial clock.  
The DFSDM supports providing an external output clock signal to drive the clock input of an external ΣΔ  
modulator. This output clock signal is delivered via the CKOUT pin. The frequency of the output clock signal  
must fall within the range specified in the device manual and can be derived from either the DFSDM clock or the  
audio clock (refer to the CKOUTSRC bit in the R32_DFSDM_CH0CFGR1 register) through a divider. The  
programmable range of the divider is 2 to 256 (CKOUTDIV bit in the R32_DFSDM_CH0CFGR1 register). The  
audio clock is obtained by selecting PLL_CLK via the SYSPLL_SEL[2:0] field in the RCC configuration, which  
in turn configures PLLCLK.  

### 42.3.2 Serial Channel Transceiver

This peripheral features 2 multiplexed serial data channels that can be selected for conversion via individual filters,  
analog watchdogs, or short-circuit detectors. These serial transceivers receive data streams from external ΣΔ  
modulators. Data streams can be transmitted in SPI format (refer to the SITP bit in the R32_DFSDM_CHyCFGR1  
register).  
The channel is enabled by setting the CHEN bit in the R32_DFSDM_CHyCFGR1 register to 1.  
<strong>Channel Input Selection</strong>  
Serial inputs (data and clock signals) for the DATINy and CKINy pins can be redirected from subsequent channel  
pins. The redirection functionality for serial input channels is configured via the CHINSEL bit in the  
R32_DFSDM_CHyCFGR1 register.  
Channel redirection can be used to acquire audio data from PDM (Pulse Density Modulation) stereo microphones.  
A PDM stereo microphone contains one data signal and one clock signal, where the data signal provides  
information for the left and right audio channels (sampling occurs on the rising edge of the clock for the left  
channel and on the falling edge for the right channel).  
Configuration of PDM microphone input serial channel;  
● PDM microphone signals (data, clock) will be connected to the DFSDM input serial channel y (DATINy,  
CKOUT) pins  
● Configure channel y as follows: CHINSEL=0 (input from given channel pins: DATINy and CKINy)  
● Configure channel (y-1) (mod 2) as follows:  
CHINSEL=1 (input from subsequent channel ((y-1)+1) pins: DATINy and CKINy)
