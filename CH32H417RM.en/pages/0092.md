#### 4.5.2.6 HSEM Interrupt Enable Register (HSEM_IER)

Offset address: 0x300  

<!-- p92-table-002 (table-4-9@1) -->
<table><tr><th>Bit</th><th>Name</th><th>Access</th><th>Description</th><th>Reset value</th></tr><tr><td>[31:0]</td><td>HSEM_IER</td><td>RW</td><td style="text-align:left">HSEM channel interrupt enable register, a core-private register. Setting this bit to 1 permits interrupt to occur when the corresponding channel is unlocked by another core. It works in conjunction with the HSEM_ISR register to generate interrupts.</td><td>0</td></tr></table>


#### 4.5.2.7 HSEM Interrupt Status Register (HSEM_ISR)

Offset address: 0x308  

<!-- p92-table-003 (table-4-9@1) -->
<table><tr><th>Bit</th><th>Name</th><th>Access</th><th>Description</th><th>Reset value</th></tr><tr><td>[31:0]</td><td>HSEM_ISR</td><td>RW1Z</td><td style="text-align:left">HSEM channel interrupt status register, a core-private register. When a locked channel is unlocked by another core, the corresponding bit is set. When used in conjunction with the HSEM_IER register, it can generate an interrupt.</td><td>0</td></tr></table>


#### 4.5.2.8 HSEM Interrupt Mask Register (HSEM_ISM)

Offset address: 0x310
