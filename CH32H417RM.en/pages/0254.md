### 14.4.7 Compare/Capture Control Register 1 (TIMx_CHCTLR1) (x=1/8)

Offset address: 0x18  
The channel can be used in input (capture mode) or output (compare mode), and the direction of the channel is  
defined by the corresponding CCxS bit. The other bits of this register have different roles in input and output
