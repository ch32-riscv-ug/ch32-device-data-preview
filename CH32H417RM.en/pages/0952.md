## 42.5 DFSDM DMA Transfer

To reduce CPU intervention, DMA transfers can be used to transfer conversions to memory. DMA transfers for  
injected conversions are enabled by setting the JDMAEN bit in the R32_DFSDM_FLTxCR1 register. DMA  
transfers for regular conversions are enabled by setting the RDMAEN bit in the R32_DFSDM_FLTxCR1 register.
