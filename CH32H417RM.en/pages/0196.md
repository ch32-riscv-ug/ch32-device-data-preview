2) Sample clock  
The register operation of the module is based on the PCLK2 (HB bus) clock. The clock reference ADCCLK of the  
conversion unit is synchronized with the PCLK2. The frequency division is configured by the ADCPRE[1:0]  
domain of the RCC_CFGR0 register, and the maximum cannot exceed 80MHz.  
3) Channel configuration  
The ADC module provides 18 channel sampling sources, including 16 external channels and 2 internal channels.  
They can be configured into 2 translation groups: rule groups and injection groups. To realize the group  
conversion consisting of a series of transformations in any order on any number of channels.  
Conversion group:  
● Rule group: consists of up to 16 transformations. Regular channels and their conversion order are set in the  
ADCx_RSQRx register. The total number of translations in the rule group should be written to L [3:0] in the  
ADCx_RSQR1 register.  
● Injection group: composed of up to 4 transformations. The injection channels and their conversion order are  
set in the ADCx_ISQR register. The total number of translations in the injection group should be written to  
JL [1:0] in the ADCx_ISQR register.  
<em>Note: If the ADCx_RSQRx or ADCx_ISQR register is changed during the conversion, the current conversion is</em>  
<em>terminated and a new startup signal will be sent to ADC to convert the newly selected group.</em>  
3 internal channels:  
● Temperature sensor: Connect the ADC_IN16 channel to measure the internal temperature(TA) of the chip.  
● VREFINT internal reference voltage: Connect the ADC_IN17 channel.  
4) Calibration  
Initialize the calibration register by writing bit 1 to the RSTCAL field of the ADCx_CTLR2 register. Wait for the  
RSTCAL bit to be cleared by hardware to indicate initialization completion. Set the CAL bit to initiate the  
calibration function. Once calibration finishes, the hardware automatically clears the CAL bit and stores the  
calibration code in ADCx_RDATAR. Normal conversion operations can then commence. It is recommended to  
perform ADC calibration once during module power-up.  
The calibration voltage value can be configured by writing the CAL_VOL[1:0] bits in the ADCx_CTLR2 register.  
Set the CAL_AUTO bit to 1 to enable the hardware automatic calibration function.  
<em>Note: 1. Before starting the calibration, you must ensure that the ADC module is powered on (ADON=1) for at</em>  
<em>least 2 ADC clock cycles.</em>  
<em>2. During ADC calibration, the sampling time must be at least 1μs.</em>  
<em>3. When calibrating the ADC, the ADC channel gain must be set to×1.</em>  
5) Programmable sample time  
ADC uses several ADCCLK cycles to sample the input voltage, and the number of sampling cycles of the  
channels can be changed by the SMPx [2:0] bit in the ADCx_SAMPTR1 and ADCx_SAMPTR2 registers. Each  
channel can be sampled at different times.  
The total conversion time is calculated as follows:  
TCONV= Sampling time + 12.5TADCCLK
