## 42.3 Function Description


### 42.3.1 DFSDM Reset and Clock

DFSDM Switch Control  
Global enablement of the DFSDM interface is achieved by setting the DFSDMEN bit in the  
R32_DFSDM_CH0CFGR1 register to 1. Once the DFSDM interface is globally enabled, all configured input  
channels y (y=0/1) and digital filters DFSDM_FLTx (x=0/1) will begin operation when their respective enable bits  
(channel enable bit CHEN in R32_DFSDM_CHyCFGR1 and DFSDM_FLTx enable bit DFEN in  
R32_DFSDM_FLTxCR1) are set to 1.  
The digital filter DFSDM_FLTx (x=0/1) is enabled by setting DFEN in the R32_DFSDM_FLTxCR1 register to 1.  
After enabling DFSDM_FLTx (DFEN=1), the Sincx digital filter unit and integrator unit will be reinitialized.
