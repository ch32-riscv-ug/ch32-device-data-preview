#### 4.2.1.22 Trigger Channel Selection Register (TSELECT)

CSR address: 0x7A0

<em>Note: (1) Both Core 1 and Core 2 implement four-channel triggers. The trigger channel is configured via the</em>  
<em>TSELECT register, where the lower two bits of TSELECT are active, while the remaining bits are permanently set</em>  
<em>to 0.</em>  
- <em>(2) The TSELECT register of Core 0 is only applicable to chips where the fifth bit of the lot number is not 0.</em>
