For example, the R32_LTDC_BPCR register is configured as 0x000E0005 (AVBP[10:0] is 0x5), and the  
R32_LTDC_AWCR register is configured as 0x028E01E5 (AAH[10:0] is 0x1E5). To configure the vertical  
position of a 630x460 window (with a vertical starting offset of 8 lines in the valid data area):  
1) First line of the layer window: WVSTPOS[10:0] should be programmed to 0xE (0x5 + 1 + 0x8);  
2) Last line of the layer window: WVSPPOS[10:0] should be programmed to 0x1DA.
