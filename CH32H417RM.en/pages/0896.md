<em>Note: PSRAM (CRAM) exhibits unpredictable data latency durations due to internal refresh cycles. Consequently,</em>  
<em>these memories emit NWAIT signals throughout the delay phase to extend latency as required. For PSRAM</em>  
<em>(CRAM), the DATLAT field must be set to 0. This instructs the FMC to immediately exit the delay phase,</em>  
<em>commence sampling NWAIT from the memory, and initiate read or write operations once the memory is ready.</em>  
<em>This method also applies to the latest generation of synchronous Flash. Unlike earlier Flash types, these Flash</em>  
<em>devices generate an NWAIT signal (refer to the specific Flash datasheet for details).</em>
