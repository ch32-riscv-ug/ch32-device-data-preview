If the RX-FIFO is full and a new data byte is received on the I3C bus, an overflow of the RX-FIFO will be  
reported (ERRF=1 in the R32_I3C_EVR register and DOVR=1 in the R32 _ I3C _ STATR register) and a  
corresponding interrupt will be generated (if enabled).  
When the transmission is completed (FCF=1, GRPF=1 or DEFF=1 in the R32_I3C_EVR register):  
● RX-FIFO is empty  
● Before the software has finished processing the RX data buffer corresponding to completed private  
write/DEFTGTS/DEFGRPA transfers (i.e., before the software has cleared the corresponding flags by  
writing CFCF/CDEFF/CGRPF to the R32_I3C_CEVR register), receiving the next private write/ DEFTGTS  
CCC/DEFGRPA CCC is received and the hardware must write data bytes to the RX-FIFO, an RX-FIFO  
overflow error will be reported (ERRF=1 in the R32_I3C_EVR register and DOVR=1 in the  
R32_I3C_STATER register) and a corresponding interrupt will be generated (if enabled).  
When the I3C peripheral is not working, the configuration of RX-FIFO management can be modified.
