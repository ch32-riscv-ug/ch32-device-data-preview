<strong>WAIT management in asynchronous access</strong>  
If the asynchronous memory sends a WAIT signal indicating that it is not ready to receive or provide data, the  
ASYNCWAIT bit in the R32_FMC_BCRx register must be set.  
If the WAIT signal is in an active state (the level depends on the WAITPOL bit), the second access phase (data  
establishment phase) controlled by the DATAST bit will be extended until WAIT becomes invalid. Unlike the data  
establishment phase, the first access phase (address establishment and address holding phase) controlled by  
ADDSET and ADDHLD bits is insensitive to WAIT, so the first access phase will not be extended.
