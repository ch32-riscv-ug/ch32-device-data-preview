<strong>● Forbidden state</strong>  
Operating Conditions: After the I3C peripheral is reset (by setting bit 1 of I3CRST in the RCC module), the  
peripheral enters a disabled state.  
Transition from disabled to idle state: When the software sets bit 1 of the R32_I3C_CFGR register&#x27;s EN field,

configuration parameters.  
<strong>● Forbidden state</strong>  
In the idle state, the software can partially update the I3C peripheral configuration.  
Transition from idle state to working state:  
1) Software-controlled triggering:  
In master mode, triggered when frame transmission starts via software write operation setting TSFSET bit 1 in the  
R32_I3C_CFGR register;  
In slave mode, triggered when software issues an IBI/CR/HJ request;  
2) Hardware Event Triggering:  
The master device triggers upon receiving a start request and maximum TCAS time from the slave device during  
bus arbitration;  
The slave device triggers upon detecting broadcast/direct CCC or private read/write operations via the SDA/SCL  
lines.  
<strong>● Working state</strong>  
In the working state, the peripheral performs transmission on the bus.  
In the working state, when the requested transmission is completed, the software will be notified by the event in  
the R32_I3C_EVR register, and the corresponding interrupt will be enabled through the R32_I3C_INTENR  
register. After the peripheral is switched from the working state to the idle state, it can still communicate on the  
bus and can be partially reconfigured.  
1) As master device: in the working state, the event/flag generated after the requested transmission is completed  
can be Frame Complete (FCF), IBI/ Master Device Role/Hot Join Request Complete (IBIF/CRF/HJF) or  
Transmission Error (ERRF);  
2) As slave device: During operation, events/flags generated upon completion of a requested transmission may  
include Dynamic Address Allocation Complete (DAUPDF), IBI Complete (IBIENDF), Controller Role  
Acquisition Complete (CRUPDF), Broadcast/Direct CCC Complete (xxUPDF/RSTF/GETF/STAF), Private  
Read/Write Complete (FCF), or Transmission Error (ERRF).
