flag and monitor the interrupt or event. When there is an interrupt or event, the interrupt control platform will  
wake the core to execute the interrupt and other programs.  

### 4.7.1 PFIC Controller Main characteristics

● Up to 256 interrupt arbitrations are supported, and the lower 32 bits are private interrupts.  
● Shared interrupt allocation  
● Support Hardware Prologue/Epilogue (HPE) without instruction overhead.  
● Provides 4-channel of Vector Table Free (VTF)  
● Support low-power sleep and wake-up management  
● The core RISC-V3F supports configurable level 2 interrupt nesting at most, and the core RISC-V5F supports  
configurable level 8 interrupt nesting at most.
