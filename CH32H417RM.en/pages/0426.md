### 22.3.7 I3C Low-power Mode

I3C in low-power mode is not affected when it is in sleep mode, and I3C interrupt can make the device exit sleep  
mode.  
When in low power mode, changes on the SCL and SDA buses wake up the system and exit low power mode.
