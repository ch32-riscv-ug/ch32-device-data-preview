<em>Note:</em>  
<em>1. VDD12A and VDDK current is large, consider the PCB alignment voltage drop loss, if the external power supply is</em>

<em>2. VREFP external capacitance to be as close as possible, otherwise affect the ADC performance.</em>

<em>For CH32V416 chip, voltage relationship: VDD33= VDD33A; and VDD33A≥ VREFP.</em>
