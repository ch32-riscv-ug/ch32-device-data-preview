Output Drive Current Characteristics  
The GPIOs (General-purpose Input/Output Ports) can absorb or output up to ±8mA of current and absorb or  
output ±20mA of current (not strictly up to VOL/VOH). In user applications, the total current driven by all IO pins  
must not exceed the absolute maximum ratings given in section 3.2:
