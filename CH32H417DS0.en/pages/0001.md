# Overview

CH32H417 is an interconnected general-purpose microcontroller designed based on QingKe RISC-V5F and  
RISC-V3F dual cores. CH32H417 integrates USB 3.2 Gen1 controller and transceiver, 100M Ethernet MAC and  
PHY, SerDes high-speed isolated transceiver, Type-C/PD controller and PHY, provides SD/EMMC controller  
SDMMC, 500MBytes universal high-speed interface UHSIF, DVP, SWPMI, PIOC, FMC, DFSDM, LTDC,  
GPHA, DMA Controller, multi-timers, 8 UARTs, I3C, 4 groups of I2C, 2 groups of QSPI, 4 groups of SPI, 2  
groups of I2S, 3 groups of CAN and other peripheral resources. Built-in 5M sampling rate dual 12-bit ADC unit,  
20M sampling rate 10-bit high-speed HSADC unit, 16-channel touch-key, dual DAC unit, 3 groups of OPA,  
voltage CMP and other analog resources, support 10M/100M Ethernet communication. It supports USB 2.0 and  
USB 3.0, USB Host and USB Device, Type-C and PDUSB fast charging, SerDes high-speed isolation and remote  
transmission, and dual-core division of labor to improve the efficiency of network protocol processing and  
communication response speed.
