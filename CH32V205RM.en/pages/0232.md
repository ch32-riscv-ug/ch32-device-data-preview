### 16.4.6 Low Byte of Program Counter PC (SFR_PRG_COUNT)

The program counter (PC) of the eMCU is 11 bits wide. The lower 8 bits of the PC can be modified by reading or  
writing SFR_PRG_COUNT, but this does not affect the upper 3 bits. This allows for short jumps and lookup table  
operations within a 256-byte range.
