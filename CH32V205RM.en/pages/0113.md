### 11.2.3 DMA Request Mapping

The DMA controller provides 16 channels, with DMA1 containing 8 channels and DMA2 containing 8 channels.  
Each channel corresponds to multiple peripheral requests. By setting the corresponding DMA control bits in the  
respective peripheral registers, the DMA function for each peripheral can be enabled or disabled independently.  
<em>Note: The entries marked with (*) in Figures 11-1 and 11-2 (Tables 11-2 and 11-3) are controlled by the</em>  
<em>EXTEN_CTLR1 register. The DMA channels to be mapped for peripheral functions can be selected via the</em>  
<em>EXTEN_CTLR1 register.</em>
