<em>Note: Unlike regular conversion sequences, if the length of JL[1:0] is less than 4, the sequence order of</em>  
<em>conversions starts with (4-JL).</em>  
<em>For example, when JL[1:0]=3 (4 injected transitions in the sequencer), the ADC will convert channels in the</em>  
<em>following order: JSQ1[4:0], JSQ2[4:0], JSQ3[4:0], and JSQ4[4:0];</em>  
<em>When JL[1:0]=2 (3 injected transitions in the sequencer), the ADC will convert the channels in the following</em>  
<em>order: JSQ2[4:0], JSQ3[4:0] and JSQ4[4:0];</em>  
<em>When JL[1:0]=1 (2 injected conversions in the sequencer), the ADC converts the channels in the following order:</em>  
<em>first JSQ3[4:0], then JSQ4[4:0];</em>  
<em>When JL[1:0] = 0 (1 injection conversion in the sequencer), the ADC will convert only the JSQ4[4:0] channels.</em>  
<em>If ADCx_ISQR[21:0]=10 00111 00011 00111 00010, the ADC will convert channels in the following order:</em>  
<em>JSQ2[4:0], JSQ3[4:0], and JSQ4[4:0], indicating that the scan conversions are performed in the following</em>  
<em>channel order: 7, 3, 7.</em>
