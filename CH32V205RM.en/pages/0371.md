### 24.2.4 NAND FLASH controller

FSMC supports 8bit and 16bit operation of NAND FLASH. FSMC can generate multiple address cycles as  
needed. In theory, FSMC has no limit to the capacity of NAND FLASH. The read and write parameters of NAND  
FLASH can be configured by software, see Table 24-26.
