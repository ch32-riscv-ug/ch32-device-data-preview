# Chapter 5 System Timer (SysTick)

QingKe V5 series microprocessor is designed with a 32-bit or 64-bit counter (SysTick) inside. Its clock source  
is the system clock or its 8-frequency division. It can provide time base, timing and measuring time for real-  
time operating system.
