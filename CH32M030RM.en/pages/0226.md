The data buffer modes of USB endpoints 1-7 are configured by the combination of RB_UEPn_RX_EN,  
RB_UEPn_TX_EN and RB_UEPn_BUF_MOD respectively. See Table 16-4 for details. Among them, in the  
double 64-byte buffer mode, the first 64-byte buffer will be selected according to RB_UEP_*_TOG=0, and the  
last 64-byte buffer will be selected according to RB_UEP_*_TOG=1. Setting Rb _ UEP _ * _ TOG = 1 can realize  
automatic switching. Note: During synchronous transmission, when (RB_UEPn_RX_EN, RB_UEPn_TX_EN,  
RB_UEPn_BUF_MOD) are only (1,0,0) or (0,1,0), endpoint 3 supports a maximum of 1023 bytes.
