GPIO (General-Purpose Input/Output) pins can handle input or output currents of up to ±8mA, and can handle  
input or output currents of ±20mA (without strictly adhering to VOL/VOH). In user applications, the total driving  
current of all I/O pins cannot exceed the absolute maximum ratings given in Section 3.2:

<em>Note: In the above conditions, if multiple I/O pins are driven at the same time, the total current cannot exceed</em>  
<em>the absolute maximum ratings given in Table 3.2. In addition, when multiple I/O pins are driven at the same</em>  
<em>time, the current on the power/ground point is very large, which will cause the voltage drop to make the</em>  
<em>internal I/O voltage not reach the power supply voltage in the table, resulting in the drive current being less</em>  
<em>than the nominal value.</em>
