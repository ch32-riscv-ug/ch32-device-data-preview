# CH32V004 Datasheet

V1.9  

# Overview

CH32V004 is a general-purpose microcontroller designed based on QingKe RISC-V core, supporting 48MHz  
system main frequency, with wide voltage, low-power consumption, 1-wire SDI and other features. Its pins and  
functions are compatible with CH32V003. CH32V004 has a built-in 12-bit ADC, with a sampling rate of up to  
3Msps; it provides rich peripheral resources, such as 7-channel DMA controller, multi-group timer, USART, I2C,  
SPI and so on.
