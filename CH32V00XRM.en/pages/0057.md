<em>2. NMI and EXC support interrupting pending cleanup and setting operations, but not interrupting enable</em>  
<em>cleanup and setting operations.</em>  
<em>Note: When using the PFIC_IENRx register to mask any interrupt or the CSR register to mask global interrupts,</em>  
<em>add a &#x27;fence.i&#x27; instruction for synchronization between the core control state and the interrupt enable state.</em>
