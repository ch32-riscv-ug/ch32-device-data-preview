### 12.3.9 Complementary Outputs and Deadtime

TIM2 is capable of outputting 2 complementary signals with dead-time (OCx and OCxN), which are  
independently polarity-controlled by the DTx_P and DTxN_P bits, and the size of the dead-time can be set by  
setting the DTx bit.
