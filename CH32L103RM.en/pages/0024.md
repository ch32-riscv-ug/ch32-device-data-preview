Activate the clock security system by setting CSSON position 1 of the RCC_CTLR register. At this point, the  
clock monitor will be enabled after the HSE oscillator startup (HSERDY=1) delay and turned off after the HSE  
clock is turned off. Once the HSE clock fails during the operation of the system, the HSE oscillator will be turned  
off, the clock failure event will be sent to the brake input of the advanced timer (TIM1), and the clock security  
interrupt will be generated, CSSF position 1, and the application will enter the NMI unshielded interrupt. By  
setting the CSSC bit, the CSSF bit flag can be cleared and the NMI interrupt suspension bit can be revoked.  
If the current HSE is the system clock, or the current HSE is the PLL input clock and the PLL is the system clock,  
the clock security system will automatically switch the system clock to the HSI oscillator and turn off the HSE  
oscillator and PLL in the event of a HSE failure.
