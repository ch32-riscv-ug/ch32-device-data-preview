### 23.2.8 CMP Low-power Mode

Setting the LPx bit, CMPx enters the low-power mode, the current corresponds to a reduction of about 3.2μA, and  
it will prolong the comparison time of CMP and increase the output offset voltage. Please refer to the  
CH32L103DS0 manual for the corresponding parameter values.
