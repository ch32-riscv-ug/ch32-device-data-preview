<em>USBD_COUNTx_TX is mapped to USB_COUNTx_RX_0</em>  
<em>USBD_COUNTx_RX is mapped to USB_COUNTx_RX_1</em>  
<em>The upper 6 bits of the USBD_COUNTx_RX register define the size of the receive packet buffer so that the</em>  
<em>USBD module can detect the overflow boundary of the buffer. The size of the buffer can be expressed</em>  
<em>according to the parameter maxPacketSize in the endpoint descriptor during the device enumeration</em>  
<em>process.</em>
