# Chapter 1 Memory and Bus Architecture


## 1.1 Bus Architecture

The CH32F20x is a microcontroller designed based on the ARM○RCortexTM-M3 core. The core, arbitration  
unit, DMA module, and SRAM memory, etc. in the framework interact through multiple sets of buses.  
The CH32V20x, CH32V30x and CH32V31x are general microcontrollers designed based on the RISC-V  
instruction set. The core, arbitration unit, DMA module and SRAM memory, etc. of the architecture interact  
through multiple sets of buses.
