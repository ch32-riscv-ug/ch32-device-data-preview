<em>Note: 1. Interrupts of NMI, HardFault, ECALL-M, ECALL-U and BREAKPOINT are always enabled by</em>

<em>2. ECALL-M, ECALL-U and BREAKPOINT each is 1 case of EXC. The status is indicated by bit3 in the</em>  
<em>EXC.</em>  
<em>3. NMI and EXC both support interrupt pending clear and set operation, but they do not support interrupt</em>  
<em>enable clear and set operation.</em>

<em>interrupt enable clear and set operation.</em>

<em>interrupts, add an additional &#x27;fence.i&#x27; instruction to synchronize the kernel control state with the interrupt</em>  
<em>enable state.</em>
