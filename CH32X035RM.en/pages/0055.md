Among them, address alignment and protection region range selection, for A_ADDR ≤ region &lt; B_ADDR region  
for memory protection (requiring both A_ADDR and B_ADDR to be 4-byte aligned):  
1. If B_ADDR - A_ADDR == 22, the NA4 method is used;  
2. If B_ADDR - A_ADDR == 2(G+2), G ≥ 1 and A_ADDR is 2(G+2) aligned then the NAPOT method is used;  
3. Otherwise the TOR method is used.
