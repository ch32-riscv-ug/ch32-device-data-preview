### 9.5.4 Dedicated CSR Registers

Some control and status registers (CSR) are defined in the RISC-V architecture to configure or identify or record  
the running status. CSR registers are internal registers within the core and use a dedicated 12-bit address space.  
<em>Note: Such registers marked with the &#x27;MRW, MRO, MRW1&#x27; attribute need to be accessed by the system in machine</em>
