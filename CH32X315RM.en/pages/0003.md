# Chapter 1 Memory and Bus Architecture


## 1.1 Bus Architecture

This series of chips is an industrial-grade general-purpose microcontroller designed based on the QingKe  
RISC-V3F core. The core, arbitration unit, DMA module, SRAM storage and other parts of its architecture  
interact through multiple sets of buses. The system block diagram is shown in Figure 1-1-1 and Figure 1-1-2.
