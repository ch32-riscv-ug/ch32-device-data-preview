# CH32M030 Datasheet

V1.2  

# Overview

CH32M030 series is an industrial-grade motor microcontroller designed based on QingKe RISC-V3B core.  
CH32M030 has built-in 4 OPAs and 3 CMPs, which support combining into 2 sets of AC small-signal  
amplification decoder QII and 2 sets of differential input current sampling ISP; built-in USB PHY and PD PHY,  
which support USB Host and USB Device function, PDUSB, Type-C fast charging function, BC1.2 and  
DCP/CDP and other high-voltage charging protocols; built-in 4 pairs of N-type power tubes gate pre-driver, to  
provide high-voltage I/O; built-in programmable current-flooding module; to provide DMA controller, 12-bit  
ADC, multi-group timer, UART serial port, I2C, SPI and other peripherals resources, provide over-voltage  
protection and over-temperature protection.
