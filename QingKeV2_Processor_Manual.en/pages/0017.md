# Chapter 4 System Timer (SysTick)

QingKe V2 series microprocessor is designed with a 32-bit plus counter (SysTick) inside, and its clock  
source can be the system clock or 8 divisions of the system clock. It can provide time base for real time  
operating system, provide timing, measure time, etc. The timer involves four registers and maps to the  
peripheral address space for controlling the SysTick, as shown in Table 4-1 below.
