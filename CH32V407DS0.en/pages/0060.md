Output drive current characteristics  
GPIO (General-Purpose Input/Output) pins can handle input or output currents of up to ±8mA, and can handle  
input or output currents of ±20mA (without strictly adhering to VOL/VOH). In user applications, the total driving  
current of all I/O pins cannot exceed the absolute maximum ratings given in Section 3.2:
