## 1.3 Clock Tree

4 groups of clock sources are introduced into the system: internal high-frequency RC oscillator (HSI),  
internal low-frequency RC oscillator (LSI), external high-frequency oscillator (HSE), and external  
low-frequency oscillator (LSE).
