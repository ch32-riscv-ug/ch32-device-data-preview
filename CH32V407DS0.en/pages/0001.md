# Overview

CH32V407 is an industrial-grade interconnected microcontroller based on the QingKe RISC-V core. It supports  
zero-wait operation at a 200MHz clock speed and the V-vector instruction set, making it suitable for applications  
such as accelerated computing and parallel processing. The CH32V407 integrates 2 sets of USB2.0 high-speed  
PHY transceivers (480Mbps), an Ethernet MAC controller, and 10/100Mbps physical layer transceivers; It  
features integrated analog resources such as dual ADCs, dual DACs, and OPAs; it also provides a wealth of  
peripheral resources, including 2 sets of DMA controllers (totaling 24 channels), SDIO, DVP interfaces, RNG,  
ARGB, LTDC, FSMC, multiple timer units, 10 USARTs, I2C interfaces, I3Cs, 3 SPIs, 2 I2Ss, and CAN  
interfaces.  
The CH32V467 adds 4MB or 8MB of on-chip extended PSRAM memory to the CH32V407 and supports  
DMA.
