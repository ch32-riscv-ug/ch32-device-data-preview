### 1.4.3 On-chip Extended PSRAM Memory

On the basis of CH32V407, CH32V467 chip also has built-in extended PSRAM memory of 4M or 8M bytes,  
which supports data storage through address and reading and writing instructions and DMA. Its main features  
include:  
● 8-bit hardware width, supporting 8-bit, 16-bit, or 32-bit random or block reads and writes  
● Support clock rates up to 300MHz, supports both rising and falling edges, and supports data transfer  
rates up to 600Mbps  
● Support write data masking  
● Support high-speed read operations via data gating  
● Support register-configurable write and read initial delays  
● Support semi-sleep mode and deep sleep mode  

### 1.4.4 Power Supply Scheme

- (1) CH32V407
● VDD= 2.9~3.6V: It supplies power to the I/O pins and the system voltage regulator (LDO), including  
power to the integrated USB PHY and Ethernet PHY. At the same time, VDD supplies power to the  
integrated system voltage regulator (LDO) and outputs a regulated power supply on the VDDK pin. The  
VDD pin adjacent to the Ethernet signal pins is the main VDD pin; it is recommended to connect a 0.1uF  
high-frequency capacitor in parallel with at least 4.7uF of capacitance. For the other VDD pins, it is  
recommended to connect a 0.1uF high-frequency capacitor. When used for USB 2.0 or Ethernet, the  
recommended VDD range is 3.15 to 3.45V. When the chip enters stop mode (LDO configured as low  
power mode) or enters standby mode, VDD cannot be lower than 3.0V.  
● VDDA= 2.9~3.6V: Supplies power to the analog portion of the high-frequency RC oscillator, ADC, OPA,  
DAC and PLL. It is recommended to externally connect a high frequency capacitor with a capacity of  
0.1uF.  
● VREF+= 2.4~3.6V: For the positive reference voltage used by the ADC, it is recommended to connect an  
external high-frequency capacitor with a capacitance of 0.1uF. The VREF+ voltage must not exceed the  
VDDA voltage.  
● VREF-: Negative reference voltage for the ADC; it is recommended to connect this pin to VSS.  
● VDDK= 1.17~1.27 V: Supplies power to the core circuitry; it is recommended to connect a 0.1uF  
capacitor in parallel with a 2.2uF high-frequency capacitor. During normal operation, the VDDK voltage  
is generated by the system voltage regulator (LDO) powered by VDD. To reduce the heat generated by  
the chip’s internal LDO, this power supply may be sourced externally (e.g., from an external DC-DC  
converter); the recommended external supply voltage should be slightly higher than the internal LDO  
output voltage.  
● VBAT= 1.8~3.6V: Optional backup power supply. When VDD and VDDA is turned off, the internal power  
switch switches to VBAT to provide power exclusively to the RTC and external low-frequency oscillator.  
The power pins with the same names above must be shorted. The voltage relationship is: VDD= VDDA; and  
VDDA≥ VREF+.
