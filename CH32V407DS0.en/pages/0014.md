### 1.4.14 Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC)

The chip integrates 2×12-bit voltage-output digital-to-analog converters (DACs) that convert two digital  
signals into two analog voltage signals for output. It supports independent or synchronous conversion of the  
dual DAC channels, 12-bit data left-aligned or right-aligned, 12-bit or 8-bit data, and external event-triggered  
conversion. It can generate triangular waves and noise. It supports DMA functionality.  

### 1.4.15 Timer and Watchdog

The timers in the system include advanced-control timers, general-purpose timers, basic timers, watchdog  
timers, and system clock timers. The number of timers included varies across different products in the series;  
please refer to Table 1-1 for details.
